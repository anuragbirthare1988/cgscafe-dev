The Journey

From quiet experiments to a thoughtfully built space

The Beginning

A Curious Start

It began with a simple question — could a daily brew be made smoother and more refined? Small kitchen trials quickly grew into a daily pursuit of flavor balance.

The Process

Refining the Craft

Night after night, ratios and textures were perfected. Using carefully chosen ingredients across our signature coffees, warm grills, and mocktail shots, every recipe found its place.

The Shift

From Experiment to Idea

This evolved beyond simple testing. It became a distinct philosophy of food and beverage — smooth, balanced, and quietly unique across our entire menu range.

The Space

CGS Comes to Life

That vision became CGS (Coffee, Grill & Shots) in Indore — a welcoming cafe where every single detail reflects care, attention, and consistency.

Today

More Than Just a Café

Today, CGS is a destination for quality without unnecessary complexity. Whether you enjoy a signature coffee or an artisan grill, every offering carries that same core dedication.
