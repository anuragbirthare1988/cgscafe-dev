Privacy & Workplace Policy

Effective Date: Nov 01, 2026

"Your trust and safety are the foundation of everything we do — online and on the floor."

This document outlines how CGS (Coffee, Grill & Shots) manages data privacy alongside our core workplace and on-site operational standards.

Workplace & On-Site Policy

  • Code & Appearance: Professional attire with non-slip footwear and hair containment across all floor and kitchen roles.
  • Operations: Structured workflows, FIFO/FEFO inventory control, and real-time workstation sanitation.
  • Service Standards: Immediate reception, order accuracy verification, and rapid table turnover.
  • Safety & Security: 24/7 CCTV surveillance, comprehensive first aid, late-shift buddy exits, and zero harassment tolerance.

Information Collection & Use

We only collect basic information (such as your name, contact number, or email) when you contact us directly or apply for open roles. Information shared with us is used strictly to respond to enquiries, process orders, or evaluate employment applications. We do not sell or rent personal data to third parties.

Data Security & Surveillance

We employ appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information. On-site areas are under continuous CCTV monitoring strictly for physical security and operational safety.

Third-Party Services & Rights

Our site may link to external services like Google Maps or social media platforms. You retain the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data we hold by contacting us at coffeegrillandshots@gmail.com.

Contact Us

For questions regarding our privacy practices or workplace policies, please reach out via our contact page or official email.
