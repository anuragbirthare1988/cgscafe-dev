Behind the Craft

Forged on the Road, Refined in the Kitchen

A look behind the vision of CGS. Built from a 16-year journey in tech, long road trips across coastlines, and an uncompromising obsession with quality ingredients.

— Anurag Birthare, Founder
Chapter 01

The Journey & The Spark

For 16 years in the fast-paced world of tech, coffee and quick bites were never just breaks—they were essential moments of connection. As a lifelong foodie whose passion doubled alongside a partner who was already a master of the craft, life naturally turned toward exploration.

From long drives tracing coastlines, cliffs, and valleys to observe nature’s flawless detailing, we found ourselves constantly studying hospitality and service. CGS was born from that exact pursuit.

Chapter 02

The Obsession

True quality starts with absolute integrity. We genuinely care about the health of our community, which is why we obsess over every single ingredient. We read between the lines so you never have to wonder what's on your plate.

Cleanliness and hygiene are non-negotiable baselines—never an afterthought. If it doesn't meet a standard we would proudly serve our own family, it doesn't leave the kitchen.

Chapter 03

The Vision

We didn't just open a cafe; we engineered an experience. Long before our doors opened, our menus were drafted, tested, and refined over and over again to serve the uncommon.

When you open our digital menu or walk into our space, every single detail reflects an intentional, extra effort designed to Jumpstart Your Mood.
