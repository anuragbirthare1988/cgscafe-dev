About CGS

What is CGS?

CGS stands for Coffee, Grill & Shots — a welcoming space focused on craft and quality. We specialize in thoughtfully formulated coffee preparations, well-executed grilled sandwiches, and refreshing beverages served in a relaxed, honest environment.

What makes CGS different from other cafes?

We craft our entire coffee experience around bespoke, carefully curated custom blends and meticulous preparation techniques—focusing entirely on richness, aroma, and consistency rather than conventional setups. Paired with our welcoming ambience, CGS delivers a truly distinct flavor profile.

Menu & Ordering

Do you offer online ordering or delivery?

Currently, CGS is a dine-in experience only. We focus on freshly prepared items served at the perfect temperature and presentation. We do not offer online sales at this time.

Are there different serving sizes available?

Yes! Select cold beverages including Cold Coffee, Frappes, and Shakes are available in multiple sizes. Check our current menu or ask our team for the available options and size indicators.

Do you serve non-vegetarian food?

Our current menu is vegetarian-friendly. We offer a variety of grilled sandwiches, pasta, and salads crafted with fresh, wholesome ingredients.

Can I customise my order?

We appreciate your preferences! While our recipes are carefully calibrated, you can request adjustments like sugar level or milk type. Please speak with our staff for available modifications.
